Hi! I am an AI Engineer at [AIRA Matrix](https://www.airamatrix.com/) in Mumbai, India, where I work at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Non-Clinical Toxicological Studies. I am currently working on the [AIRATox Project](https://www.airamatrix.com/products/airatox), where we are developing and validating histopathology pipelines across multiple preclinical organs, studies, and laboratories. Recently, my work on automated analysis and robust representation learning for toxicological studies has led to a [MIDL 2026](https://2026.midl.io/) publication and a poster presentation at [JSTP 2026](https://cfmeeting.com/jstp42/en/). 

I graduated in 2024 with a Dual Degree in [Engineering Physics](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/dept/phy) from the [Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi](https://iitbhu.ac.in/). Apart from my work at AIRA Matrix, I am interested in building better AI systems and training more robust models to improve performance and generalization in medical AI, with a particular focus on generative modeling, out-of-distribution generalization, and histopathological foundation models.

Previously, I worked as a Research Intern at the [Medical and Environmental Computing (MEC) Lab](https://www.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/gris/forschung_1/medical_computing/index.en.jsp) at TU Darmstadt, Germany, where we developed robust 3D cerebral ventricle segmentation models for clinical CT volumes affected by intra-ventricular hemorrhage (IVH), addressing the challenge of blood-induced boundary ambiguity. I was also involved in developing GeoLighting, an end-to-end, three-stage computer vision pipeline for automatically discovering and geo-localizing streetlights from GPS-tagged street-view videos, enabling scalable streetlight map generation in collaboration with [iHub-Data](https://ihub-data.iiit.ac.in/), IIIT Hyderabad, India.
